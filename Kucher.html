Simon-Kucher strengthens Transaction Services & Private Equity leadership in the Americas with appointments of Andrew Kutter and Owen Tindle

September 15, 2026

Simon-Kucher, the world’s leading commercial growth and pricing consultancy, today announced the appointment of Andrew Kutter and Owen Tindle and as Partners and co-leaders of its Transaction Services & Private Equity practice in the Americas. 

Kutter and Tindle bring complementary sector expertise and extensive experience advising investors and management teams across a diverse range of complex client mandates. As co-leaders, they will expand Simon-Kucher’s Transaction Services & Private Equity capabilities in the Americas and collaborate across the firm to support clients throughout the investment lifecycle.

“These appointments are part of the broader growth and momentum we’re seeing across Simon-Kucher in North America,” said Brad Soper, Managing Partner of the Americas at Simon-Kucher. “We continue to invest in exceptional talent and strengthen our capabilities as we expand our business across the region.”

Based in Simon-Kucher’s San Francisco office, Kutter brings more than 20 years of consulting experience and has advised clients on several hundred transactions. He has particular expertise in industrials and business services, with a focus on assessing market attractiveness, competitive dynamics, customer behavior, and commercial performance.

“Simon-Kucher’s ability to connect deep commercial expertise with the priorities of investors and management teams creates a distinctive proposition in the market,” said Kutter. “I look forward to broadening our impact across the Americas and helping clients translate investment opportunities into commercial results.”

Based in Simon-Kucher’s Boston office, Tindle brings more than 18 years of transaction advisory experience and has advised clients on nearly 500 transactions, with a strong focus on technology and technology-enabled business services. His work spans buy-side and sell-side engagements, including market assessment, customer insights, competitive positioning, and growth strategy.

“Simon-Kucher’s growth-focused mindset, entrepreneurial culture, and depth of expertise create a strong foundation for the continued growth of our Transaction Services & Private Equity practice,” said Tindle. “I look forward to scaling our presence, deepening sponsor relationships, and delivering measurable impact for our clients.”

The appointments come as Simon-Kucher continues to expand its Transaction Services & Private Equity business, supporting investors and management teams across the investment lifecycle in the Americas and globally.

“Andrew and Owen bring deep deals experience, having advised hundred of clients on commercial due diligence, and combining that with our existing US TSPE team and the deep value creation experience within our US business creates an extremely compelling offering for clients,” said Mark Maitland, Senior Partner and Global Head of Transaction Services & Private Equity at Simon-Kucher. “Their appointments build on the addition of four new partners to our Transaction Services & Private Equity practice in Europe over the past 15 months, reflecting the continued growth of our team globally. Together, this expanded leadership will help us strengthen our deal-side capabilities and bring Simon-Kucher’s broader commercial expertise, technology, and proprietary tools to clients around the world.”
