    RSM earns 2026-27 Microsoft AI Business Solutions Inner Circle award

    Recognition highlights RSM’s leadership across the Microsoft ecosystem and commitment to delivering AI-powered business outcomes for the middle market

    CHICAGO – (Oct. 13, 2026) – RSM US LLP ("RSM") - the leading provider of consulting, tax and assurance services for the middle market — has been recognized as a member of Microsoft's 2026-27 Inner Circle. Participation within Inner Circle is based on sales achievements that rank RSM in the top echelon of Microsoft’s AI Business Solutions global network of partners. This distinction highlights RSM's breadth across the Microsoft ecosystem, helping middle market organizations leverage AI to optimize finance and operations, strengthen customer engagement and manage risk.

    RSM's inclusion in the Inner Circle reflects the strength of its alliance with Microsoft and continued investment in business applications, data platforms and AI solutions that deliver measurable business outcomes. RSM's capabilities span Microsoft Dynamics 365, Power Platform, Microsoft Fabric and Copilot, bringing together AI, business applications and data capabilities to modernize operations and accelerate transformation.

    The acknowledgment comes at a pivotal moment in the AI marketplace. As AI adoption accelerates, many organizations are discovering that scaling AI across the enterprise is harder than deploying it. RSM's Middle Market AI Survey 2026 highlighted that only 17% of organizations were pursuing transformational initiatives due to challenges around governance, security, data quality and integration. RSM works alongside clients to address these barriers and unlock sustainable business value from AI.

    "Being part of Microsoft’s Inner Circle reflects RSM’s commitment to delivering value for middle market organizations through AI and business applications,” said Jeff Monaco, principal and Microsoft leader at RSM. “Combined with our industry experience and strong Microsoft relationship, this recognition strengthens our ability to help clients accelerate transformation."

    RSM also recently earned the Microsoft Frontier Partner Badge, further validating its leadership in AI and ability to deliver secure, scalable solutions across the Microsoft ecosystem. Together, these achievements reflect RSM’s commitment to advancing AI, data and business applications as drivers of transformation, performance and long-term value for middle market organizations.
